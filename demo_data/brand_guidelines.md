## Voice and Tone
Our voice is confident, precise, and human. Write for IT decision-makers: lead
with outcomes, not adjectives. Avoid hyperbole such as "the best" or "the most
powerful" unless a cited benchmark supports it.

## Claims and Substantiation
Every performance claim in external marketing must reference a specific,
verifiable source (benchmark name, spec sheet, or compliance document). Do not
state a battery, weight, or performance figure without a citation.

## Prohibited Language
- Do not guarantee security ("unhackable", "100% secure").
- Do not compare directly against named competitors without legal review.
- Do not use customer data or named customers without written approval.

## Audience Notes
For CIO and security-buyer audiences, emphasize manageability, supply-chain
assurance, and total cost of ownership over raw specifications.
