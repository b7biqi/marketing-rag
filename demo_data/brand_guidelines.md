## Voice and Tone
Our voice is confident, precise, and human. Write for IT decision-makers: lead
with outcomes, not adjectives. Avoid hyperbole such as "the best" or "the most
powerful" unless a cited benchmark supports it.

## Claims and Substantiation
Every performance claim in external marketing must reference a specific,
verifiable source: a benchmark name, a spec sheet, or a compliance document.
Do not state a battery, weight, or performance figure without a citation to its
source.

## Prohibited Language
- Do not guarantee security. Never use words like "unhackable" or "100% secure".
- Do not make direct comparisons against named competitors without legal review.
- Do not use customer data or name specific customers without written approval.

## Messaging Pillars
1. Manageability — fleet deployment, remote management, and firmware control.
2. Supply-chain assurance — verifiable provenance and tamper evidence.
3. Total cost of ownership — durability, serviceability, and long support life.

## Audience Notes
For CIO and security-buyer audiences, emphasize manageability, supply-chain
assurance, and total cost of ownership over raw specifications. For end-user and
prosumer audiences, lead with portability, display quality, and battery life.
