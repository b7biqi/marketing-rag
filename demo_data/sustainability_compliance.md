## 1. Environmental Certifications
The ThinkPad X1, T14, and P16 product lines are EPEAT Gold registered and
ENERGY STAR 8.0 certified for energy efficiency.

## 2. Materials and Recycled Content
Select chassis components use post-consumer recycled (PCR) plastic and recycled
aluminum. Packaging is 100% recyclable and uses plastic-free molded fiber cushions.

## 3. Hazardous Substance Compliance
All products are compliant with the EU RoHS Directive and REACH regulation, and use
low-halogen materials in accordance with IEC 61249-2-21.

## 4. Carbon and Reporting
Each product family publishes a Product Carbon Footprint (PCF) report covering
manufacturing, transport, and estimated use-phase emissions over a 4-year lifespan.

## 5. End of Life
A take-back and recycling program is available in supported regions to recover and
responsibly process end-of-life devices.
