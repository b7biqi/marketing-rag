## 1. Overview
The ThinkPad P16 Gen 3 is a 16-inch mobile workstation for engineering, 3D, and AI
development workloads. It weighs 2.95 kg and is ISV-certified for major CAD and
simulation applications.

## 2. Processor and Graphics
- Processor: Intel Core Ultra 9 285HX (workstation-class).
- Discrete graphics: NVIDIA RTX 5000 Ada Generation Laptop GPU with 16 GB GDDR6.
- The P16 is the only model in this lineup with a discrete NVIDIA professional GPU.

## 3. Memory and Storage
- Memory: up to 192 GB DDR5 ECC across four SO-DIMM slots, user-replaceable.
- Storage: up to 8 TB across dual PCIe Gen4 NVMe SSDs, RAID 0/1 capable.

## 4. Display and Power
- 16-inch 3.2K (3200 x 2000) OLED, 400 nits, 100% DCI-P3.
- 230 W slim-tip AC adapter; battery rated up to 10 hours under MobileMark 25.

## 5. Security
- Discrete Trusted Platform Module (dTPM 2.0) and self-encrypting drive options.
- Fingerprint reader and optional IR camera with privacy shutter.
