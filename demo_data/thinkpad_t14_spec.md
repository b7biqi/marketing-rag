## 1. Overview
The ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 is a mainstream business workhorse weighing 1.36 kg. It
balances performance, serviceability, and connectivity, and is tested against
12 MIL-STD-810H durability methods.

## 2. Processor and Memory
- Processor: AMD Ryzen 7 PRO 8840U with integrated Radeon 780M graphics.
- Memory: up to 64 GB DDR5, using two SO-DIMM slots that are user-replaceable.
- Storage: up to 2 TB PCIe Gen4 NVMe SSD, with a second M.2 slot on select models.

## 3. Battery and Power
Battery life is rated up to 22 hours under the MobileMark 25 benchmark.
Rapid Charge restores up to 80% capacity in approximately 60 minutes.

## 4. Display
- 14-inch WUXGA (1920 x 1200) IPS panel, 400 nits, low-power option available.
- Optional 2.8K OLED panel on premium configurations.

## 5. Ports and Connectivity
- 2x USB4 (USB-C), 2x USB-A 3.2, 1x HDMI 2.1.
- Built-in Ethernet (RJ45) port for wired enterprise networks.
- Optional Smart Card reader and 4G/5G WWAN.

## 6. Security
- Discrete Trusted Platform Module (dTPM 2.0).
- Fingerprint reader and optional IR camera with privacy shutter.

## 7. Serviceability
The T14 Gen 6 is designed for easy in-field service: the bottom cover is removable
with standard screws, and memory, storage, and the battery are user-replaceable.
