## 1. Overview
The ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 is an ultralight business laptop weighing 1.09 kg,
built on a carbon-fiber chassis tested against 12 MIL-STD-810H durability methods.

## 2. Performance
- Processor: Intel Core Ultra 7 165U.
- Memory: up to 32 GB LPDDR5x soldered memory.
- Storage: up to 2 TB PCIe Gen4 SSD.

## 3. Battery and Power
Battery life is rated up to 18 hours under the MobileMark 25 benchmark.
Rapid Charge restores up to 80% capacity in approximately 60 minutes.

## 4. Display
- 14-inch 2.8K OLED panel, 400 nits, 100% DCI-P3 color gamut.
- Optional low-power IPS panel rated at 1000 nits for outdoor use.

## 5. Security
Includes a discrete Trusted Platform Module (TPM 2.0), a fingerprint reader
integrated into the power button, and an optional human-presence-detection IR camera.
