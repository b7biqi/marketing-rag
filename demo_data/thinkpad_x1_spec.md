## 1. Overview
The ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 is an ultralight flagship business laptop weighing
1.09 kg. The chassis is built from carbon-fiber-reinforced and magnesium-alloy
materials and is tested against 12 MIL-STD-810H durability methods.

## 2. Processor and Memory
- Processor: Intel Core Ultra 7 165U with integrated Intel AI Boost NPU.
- Memory: up to 32 GB LPDDR5x, soldered to the mainboard (not user-upgradeable).
- Storage: up to 2 TB PCIe Gen4 NVMe SSD.

## 3. Battery and Power
Battery life is rated up to 18 hours under the MobileMark 25 benchmark.
Rapid Charge restores up to 80% capacity in approximately 60 minutes from a
65 W USB-C adapter.

## 4. Display
- 14-inch 2.8K (2880 x 1800) OLED panel, 400 nits, 100% DCI-P3 color gamut.
- Optional low-power IPS panel rated at 1000 nits peak brightness for outdoor use.
- Anti-reflective and anti-smudge coating on touch configurations.

## 5. Ports and Connectivity
- 2x Thunderbolt 4 (USB-C), 2x USB-A 3.2, 1x HDMI 2.1, 1x headphone/mic combo.
- No built-in Ethernet (RJ45) port; a USB-C-to-Ethernet dongle is required.
- Wi-Fi 7 and optional 5G WWAN.

## 6. Security
- Discrete Trusted Platform Module (dTPM 2.0).
- Fingerprint reader integrated into the power button (match-on-chip).
- Optional human-presence-detection IR camera with privacy shutter.

## 7. Sustainability
The X1 Carbon Gen 13 uses recycled aluminum and post-consumer recycled plastic
in select components and ships in 100% recyclable packaging.
