## 1. Base Warranty
All ThinkPad commercial laptops include a 3-year base limited warranty covering
parts and labor for manufacturing defects. The warranty period begins on the date
of original purchase.

## 2. Premier Support
Premier Support is an optional upgrade that provides a single point of contact,
24x7 access to advanced-level technicians, and end-to-end case management for both
hardware and selected software issues.

## 3. Accidental Damage Protection
Accidental Damage Protection (ADP) is an optional add-on that covers non-warranty
incidents such as drops, spills, and electrical surges. ADP does not cover theft,
loss, or intentional damage.

## 4. Onsite and Depot Service
Standard service is depot (mail-in). Optional Onsite Service dispatches a technician
to the customer location, typically next business day after remote diagnosis.

## 5. Battery Coverage
The internal battery is covered for 1 year under the base warranty, independent of
the 3-year system coverage.
